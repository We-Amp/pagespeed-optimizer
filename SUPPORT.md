# Support

mod_pagespeed 2.1 is open source (Apache-2.0) — support is what's for sale.

## Community support

- **Bug reports and feature requests:** open an issue at
  <https://github.com/We-Amp/pagespeed-optimizer/issues>. Search the existing
  issues first, and include the version you run, your platform, and the
  smallest configuration or request that reproduces the problem.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do NOT open a public issue. See
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the reporting process.

## Commercial support

Commercial support — SLA-backed help, migration assistance, and consulting —
is available from [We-Amp B.V.](https://we-amp.com).
