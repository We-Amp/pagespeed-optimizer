About

The story behind the lens

I'm Lena Voss, a landscape and nature photographer based in the Pacific Northwest. For over a decade, I've traveled to remote corners of the world to capture the quiet beauty of natural landscapes. My work focuses on the interplay of light, weather, and terrain.

Each photograph is a meditation on patience and presence. I shoot exclusively with natural light, often returning to the same location dozens of times waiting for perfect conditions. My prints are available in limited editions and have been exhibited in galleries across North America and Europe.
